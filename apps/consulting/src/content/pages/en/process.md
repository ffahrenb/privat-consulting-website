## Working together

### 1. Initial contact & scoping

You reach out and describe your situation. In an initial conversation (free of charge, approx. 30–60 minutes), I understand your needs, the context and the stakeholders involved. Together, we clarify whether and how I can support you.

### 2. Proposal

Based on the scoping, I prepare a tailored proposal. This includes objectives, the suggested approach, formats (consulting, facilitation, workshop, coaching), scope and pricing.

### 3. Delivery

Depending on the engagement, I work in individual sessions with key stakeholders, in facilitated workshops with teams or in an ongoing advisory role over a defined period. I adapt to your structures and rhythms.

### 4. Results & handover

At the end of an engagement, I document the key findings and recommendations. My goal is for your organisation to be able to continue independently after the consulting ends.

---

## Formats

**Consulting** – Ongoing support in change processes, data governance projects or strategy development. Typically over several months.

**Facilitation** – Structured workshops and working sessions with a clear objective. For teams, project groups or governance boards.

**Workshops** – Half-day or full-day formats on specific topics such as data culture, roles and responsibilities or change communication.

**Coaching** – Individual support for leaders in data-adjacent and technology-driven roles.

---

## Framework conditions

### Format
Consulting and coaching take place either **online via Zoom** or **on-site at your location**. Workshops typically take place on-site.

### Language
Consulting is available in **German** and **English**.
