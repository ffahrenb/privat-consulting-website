## Psychological support for your employees

Your employees' mental health isn't a side issue – it's the foundation of performance, collaboration and long-term success. Companies that invest in their teams' well-being benefit from fewer absences, higher motivation and stronger retention.

I offer companies an Employee Assistance Programme (EAP): confidential psychological counselling for employees – complemented by workshops and seminars on stress, burnout prevention and mindfulness.

---

## Employee Assistance Programme (EAP)

### How it works

Your company purchases an hour contingent for psychological counselling. Your employees can use this contingent for confidential individual sessions – straightforward, timely and without bureaucratic hurdles.

**Confidentiality:** All session content is subject to professional confidentiality. The company receives anonymised usage statistics on request, but never information about the content of conversations.

### Typical topics

- Stress and overload at work
- Burnout prevention
- Dealing with role conflicts and high expectations
- Change processes and uncertainty at work
- Work-life balance
- Personal crises affecting work

### Why I'm particularly suited for IT and technology companies

I know your employees' working world from my own experience. As a Product Owner Data & Analytics, I worked in agile teams, data projects and transformation processes myself. I understand the specific pressures on IT professionals, project managers, data engineers and leaders in digitalisation projects – not from a textbook, but from my own day-to-day work.

This dual perspective – psychologist and IT practitioner – enables counselling that resonates with your employees, because I speak their language and know their challenges.

## Workshops & Seminars

In addition to individual counselling, I offer tailored workshops and seminars for teams and organisations. These can be booked as part of the EAP or as a standalone offering.

### Topics

**Stress management** – How does stress arise? What maintains it? And which strategies help sustainably? A practice-oriented workshop based on insights from stress research.

**Burnout prevention** – Recognising early warning signs, identifying stress factors and developing effective prevention measures – for individuals and teams.

**Mindfulness at work** – Focus, self-awareness and stress reduction through mindfulness-based methods. Practical and applicable in everyday work.

### Formats

- Half-day or full-day workshops
- On-site at your company or online
- For teams of 5 or more
- Content and duration tailored to your company
