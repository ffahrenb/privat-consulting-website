The initial consultation is free of charge and non-binding. It lasts approx. 30 minutes and takes place online via video conference. Simply send me a brief message with your concern and preferred times.

## Crisis support

My counselling is not a crisis service. If you are in an acute crisis, please contact one of the following services:

- **Die Dargebotene Hand (The Helping Hand):** Phone 143 (24 hours, free)
- **Pro Juventute (for children and young people):** Phone 147 (24 hours, free)
- **Psychiatric emergency service:** via the medical emergency service of your canton
