## What is psychological counselling?

Psychological counselling is aimed at fundamentally healthy individuals seeking support with specific concerns – whether work-related stress, a difficult decision, a process of change or the desire for personal development. It is resource-oriented and builds on the existing strengths and abilities of the person seeking advice.

## What is psychotherapy?

Psychotherapy is the professional treatment of mental disorders with clinical significance (e.g. depression, anxiety disorders, trauma-related disorders). It requires a diagnosis according to a recognised classification system (ICD) and may only be practised in Switzerland by professionals with a recognised psychotherapy postgraduate qualification and a cantonal licence to practise.

## Distinction

My services comprise **psychological counselling, coaching and employee assistance**. I do **not offer psychotherapy** and do **not treat mental disorders with clinical significance**.

Specifically, this means:

- I do not make psychiatric or psychotherapeutic diagnoses
- I do not treat clinical conditions (e.g. depression, anxiety disorders, addiction, trauma-related disorders)
- I do not prescribe medication (this is reserved for medical doctors)
- My services do not replace medical or psychotherapeutic treatment

## Insurance coverage

As this is not psychotherapy, the costs for my services are **not covered by the mandatory basic health insurance (KVG)** in Switzerland. Some supplementary insurances may contribute to the costs of psychological counselling. Please check directly with your health insurer.

## When is psychotherapy the right choice?

If you are suffering from persistent psychological complaints that significantly impair your daily life – such as lasting sadness, severe anxiety, sleep disorders, addiction or thoughts of self-harm – I recommend contacting a psychotherapist or your GP.

Should it become apparent during our counselling that psychotherapeutic treatment would be advisable, I'm happy to support you in finding a suitable professional.
