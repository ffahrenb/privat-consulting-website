## About me

My name is Florian Fahrenbach. I'm a psychologist (FSP) and hold a doctorate in social and economic sciences. In my practice, I support individuals dealing with stress and burnout, guide people towards their personal vision, and work with mountain athletes on mental challenges.

I know the dynamics of stress and overwhelm not only from research, but also from my own experience. Psychological support has helped me through difficult phases – and this experience informs my work.

I also offer companies an Employee Assistance Programme: confidential psychological support for employees – particularly in technology-driven work environments.

---

## Who I work with

- Individuals dealing with work-related stress or seeking effective recovery strategies
- People in transitional phases – career change, re-entry or the desire for change
- Mountain athletes who want to address mental challenges
- Leaders and professionals who want to strengthen their effectiveness and lead more reflectively
- Companies that want to provide their employees with professional psychological support
