## 3. Collection and processing of personal data

### 3.1 Visiting the website

When you visit this website, technical data transmitted by your browser is automatically collected. This includes: IP address (anonymised where technically possible), date and time of access, page/URL visited, browser type and version, operating system.

This data is collected by the hosting provider (GitHub Pages) as part of technical operations. I do not have access to this data. Details on data processing by GitHub: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

### 3.2 Contact

When you contact me by email or via the contact form, the data you provide (e.g. name, email address, message) is stored to process your enquiry and to answer any follow-up questions. This data is not shared with third parties without your consent.

Data collected in the context of a contact enquiry is deleted once it is no longer needed for processing your request.

### 3.2a Electronic communication

Please note: Absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed for electronic communication (email, video conferencing). Confidential content (personal or health-related matters) should not be transmitted via unencrypted email.

### 3.3 Counselling relationship

During a counselling relationship, additional personal data may be collected (e.g. phone number, appointment scheduling, session notes). This data is used exclusively for the delivery of counselling and is subject to professional confidentiality.

Counselling-related data is deleted after the conclusion of counselling and after the expiry of statutory retention periods, unless there is a legal obligation for further retention.

### 3.4 Employee Assistance Programme

Special confidentiality rules apply to Employee Assistance Programmes. The content of counselling sessions is subject to professional confidentiality and is never disclosed to the employer. Anonymised usage statistics (e.g. number of sessions booked) can be provided to the company on request.
