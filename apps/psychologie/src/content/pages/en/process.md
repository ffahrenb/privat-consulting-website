## For individuals

### 1. Getting in touch

You contact me by email (florian.fahrenbach@gmail.com) or via the contact form. A brief description of your concern helps me prepare the initial conversation. I typically respond within two business days.

### 2. Free initial consultation

In a short, non-binding initial consultation (approx. 30 minutes, online via Zoom), we get to know each other. We clarify your concern, your expectations and whether my services are a good fit for your situation. This consultation is free of charge and without obligation.

### 3. Agreement and start

If we decide to work together, we agree on goals, framework and the first appointments. Counselling typically begins with a deeper exploration of your needs in the first regular session.

Before counselling begins, you will receive a counselling agreement and a consent form for signature.

### 4. Counselling process

Sessions take place at the agreed rhythm – typically every one to two weeks. Each session lasts 60 minutes. Between sessions, there's space for reflection and implementation.

For vocation coaching, the process typically comprises approximately 8 sessions with a clear goal: formulating your personal vision.

### 5. Conclusion

We regularly evaluate progress together. Counselling ends when the agreed goals have been achieved. There is no minimum commitment.

## For companies

### 1. Contact & needs assessment

You get in touch and describe your starting situation. In an initial conversation (free of charge, approx. 30–60 minutes), I understand your needs, context and organisational requirements.

### 2. Tailored proposal

Based on the needs assessment, I prepare a tailored proposal including the suggested model (EAP contingent, workshops or combination), scope and pricing.

### 3. Delivery

Depending on the engagement, I work in confidential individual sessions with employees, in workshops with teams or a combination of both. I adapt to your structures and rhythms.

### 4. Evaluation

On request, I provide regular anonymised reports on programme usage – without any inferences about individual participants.

## Framework conditions

### Format
Individual counselling takes place primarily **online via Zoom**. You receive the access link by email before each session. Workshops can also take place **on-site** at your company.

### Duration and frequency (individual counselling)
- **Individual session:** 60 minutes
- **Recommended rhythm:** weekly or biweekly
- **Duration of the process:** individual, typically 4–10 sessions
- **Vocation coaching:** approx. 8 sessions

### Language
Counselling is available in **German** and **English**.

### Companies

Scope and pricing for Employee Assistance Programmes and workshops are agreed individually. Get in touch – I'm happy to prepare a proposal.

### Payment
Payment by bank transfer (invoice). Cash payment for in-person appointments by arrangement.

### Cancellation policy
Appointments can be cancelled or rescheduled free of charge up to **24 hours before the session**. For later cancellations or no-shows, a flat fee of CHF 120 will be charged.

### Invoicing
Billing takes place after each session or monthly by invoice. Payment term: 30 days.

### Insurance coverage
Psychological counselling and coaching are generally **not covered by the basic health insurance (KVG)** in Switzerland. Some supplementary insurances may contribute to costs. Please check with your health insurer.

## Confidentiality

I am bound by **professional confidentiality**. All counselling content is confidential. Information is only shared with third parties if you expressly release me in writing.

For Employee Assistance Programmes: the company receives anonymised usage statistics on request, but never information about the content of conversations.
