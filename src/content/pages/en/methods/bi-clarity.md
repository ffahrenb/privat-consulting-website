## BI Clarity — The Power BI Governance Audit

**A data-driven assessment of your entire Power BI landscape. Automated deep analysis at the model, report, and workspace level — delivering clear, actionable recommendations instead of guesswork.**

---

## The Methodology — Four Phases to an Auditable BI Inventory

BI Clarity follows a structured approach that combines technical deep analysis with organizational context. Every phase produces verifiable artifacts — no black box.

### 01 — Landscape Scan

Automated retrieval of the entire Power BI landscape via the REST API: workspaces, permissions, data models, data sources, refresh schedules, and refresh status. The result is a complete inventory of all tenant objects — with zero manual effort and no access to report content required.

### 02 — Deep Extraction

Technical deep analysis at the file level: every PBIX container is automatically unpacked and examined for model structure, DAX measures, relationships, data sources, and report layouts. Encrypted or protected files are detected and safely skipped. Result: a machine-readable inventory of every single report.

### 03 — Governance Scoring

Each report is evaluated against weighted criteria: orphaned measures, missing relationships, empty visualizations, duplicates, and data source connectivity. The result is a score per report with a clear recommendation — **Keep**, **Review**, or **Archive** — with a fully transparent rationale.

### 04 — Report & Roadmap

All findings are delivered in three formats: an Excel audit report for leadership, structured JSON for technical downstream processing, and a Markdown export with Mermaid diagrams for documentation. Based on the results, we collaboratively develop a prioritized governance roadmap.

## Use Cases — When to Deploy BI Clarity

### Taming BI Sprawl

Over the years, dozens to hundreds of reports have accumulated. Nobody knows which ones are still used, which are duplicates, and where outdated data sources are connected. BI Clarity creates in days the overview that would take weeks manually.

### Preparing for Migration

Before moving to Microsoft Fabric, a new data platform, or consolidating workspaces, you need a reliable inventory. BI Clarity delivers the as-is analysis as a migration foundation — including a dependency graph and scoring.

### Optimizing License Costs

Unused reports in Premium workspaces, redundant data models, reports without visuals — all of this costs money. Governance Scoring identifies archive candidates and quantifies savings potential.

### Compliance & Audit

Who has access to which workspaces? Which data sources are connected? Are there uncontrolled data flows? BI Clarity produces an auditable inventory that withstands compliance requirements — automatically and repeatably.

### Handover & Onboarding

A BI lead leaves the company or a new team takes over. BI Clarity documents the entire landscape in machine-readable form and generates LLM-optimized exports that radically accelerate knowledge transfer.

### Setting a Quality Baseline

You want to enforce standards — for example: every report needs relationships, no orphaned measures, SQL-based sources. BI Clarity delivers the first measurement and can be used as a recurring quality check.
