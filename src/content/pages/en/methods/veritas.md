## VERITAS — ICT Hypothesis Validation Method

**Evidence-based decision-making for complex ICT challenges**

---

## The Problem

Every IT decision rests on assumptions. Should we migrate our ERP or upgrade it? Does analytics logic belong in the data warehouse or in the reporting tool? Do we need a cloud strategy or is on-premise sufficient?

In most organizations, these questions are answered by gut feeling, hierarchy, or the loudest voice in the room. The result: costly missteps, never-ending discussion loops, and projects that fail before they truly begin.

## The Solution

**VERITAS** (*Validation through Evidence-based Research of ICT Architecture & Strategy*) is a structured consulting method that makes implicit assumptions explicit — and systematically tests them against evidence.

The core idea: Instead of spending months evaluating options, we formulate the assumptions behind the decision as **testable hypotheses** — and validate them through facts, industry research, and best practices.

---

## How VERITAS Works

### Phase 0 — Context
We capture the business and project context: existing systems, stakeholders, decision drivers, and any prior work already completed.

### Phase 1 — Hypothesize
In a facilitated workshop, your decision-makers articulate the assumptions behind the pending decision — as precise, testable hypotheses. Typically 5–10.

### Phase 2 — Research
Each hypothesis is individually researched: industry analyses, vendor documentation, academic studies, reference projects, and expert assessments.

### Phase 3 — Evidence
From the research, we distill context-specific, quantified facts and findings — not opinions, but substantiated statements.

### Phase 4 — Recommend
Each hypothesis receives a concrete, actionable recommendation backed by sources and best practice examples from comparable organizations.

### Phase 5 — Open Items
What remains unclear is transparently documented. Honesty about knowledge gaps is a quality feature, not a shortcoming.

### Phase 6 — Score
Each hypothesis is rated on two axes: **Truth score** (0–100) and **Confidence in the assessment** (0–100). This yields a clear traffic-light rating:

- **Accepted** — confirmed by evidence
- **Split** — mixed evidence
- **Rejected** — contradicted by evidence
- **Inconclusive** — insufficient data

### Phase 7 — Synthesize
All results are consolidated into a decision brief: hypothesis overview with traffic-light system, conclusions, management summary, and concrete recommendations with a timeline.

## When to Use VERITAS

- **ERP decisions:** Migrate or upgrade? Which vendor? Now or later?
- **Cloud strategy:** On-premise, public cloud, hybrid? Which provider?
- **Architecture decisions:** Monolith or best-of-breed? Make or buy?
- **Analytics & BI:** Reporting landscape consolidation, data warehouse strategy
- **IT transformation:** Digitalization strategy, technology roadmap validation
- **Any ICT decision** where stakeholders hold divergent views

## About the Method

VERITAS stands for *Validation through Evidence-based Research of ICT Architecture & Strategy* — and for the Latin word for **truth**. Because ICT decisions should be built on truth, not assumptions.

The method combines the rigor of scientific hypothesis validation with the pragmatism of management consulting. It was developed and proven in real-world ICT projects.
