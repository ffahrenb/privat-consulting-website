When you visit this website, technical data transmitted by your browser is automatically collected. This includes: IP address (anonymised where technically possible), date and time of access, page/URL visited, browser type and version, operating system.

This data is collected by the hosting provider (GitHub Pages) as part of technical operations. I do not have access to this data. Details on data processing by GitHub can be found in GitHub's privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

### 3.2 Contact

When you contact me by email or via the contact form, the data you provide (e.g. name, email address, company, message) is stored to process your enquiry. This data is not shared with third parties without your consent.

### 3.3 Consulting relationship

During a consulting relationship, additional data may be collected (e.g. appointment scheduling, project documentation). This data is used exclusively for the delivery of the consulting engagement and is treated confidentially.
